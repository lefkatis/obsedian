# 🏡 HOUSE 2021 — Master Project Summary

> [!abstract] Project Overview
> Comprehensive architectural, structural, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing), smart home automation, water treatment, and town planning specifications for **HOUSE 2021**, a single-story L-shaped residence in Latsia, Nicosia.

---

## 1. General Project & Site Specifications

- **Project Name:** HOUSE 2021 (Plot / ΟΡΙΟ ΤΕΜ. 2021)
- **Clients:** Andreas & Maria
- **Architect:** Stathis Lefkatis Architecture (4 Kouriou Street, Latsia, Nicosia)
- **Interior Living Area:** $190.00\text{ m}^2$ (estimated total plot area $\approx 563.4\text{ m}^2$)
- **Architectural Concept:** Modern single-story L-shaped "Panopticon" layout separating the loud public entertainment wing from the quiet private bedroom corridor, anchored around a central courtyard veranda and swimming pool.
- **Topography & Setbacks:**
  - Natural upward slope of $\sim 1.5\text{ m}$ from the public road ($\pm 0.00\text{ m}$ to $+0.31\text{ m}$) to the finished house/pool deck level ($+1.40\text{ m}$ to $+1.60\text{ m}$).
  - Mandatory **$3.00\text{ m}$ forward building setback line** (*Οικοδομικό Όριο 3m*) along the front road boundary.

## 3. Architectural Layout & Key Dimensions

- **Open-Plan Span:**
  - Total span (far-left wall including staircase to living room TV wall): **$13.32\text{ m}$ ($43.7\text{ ft}$)**
  - Kitchen counter edge to TV wall (excluding stairs): **$11.83\text{ m}$ ($38.8\text{ ft}$)**
  - Kitchen island to TV wall: **$10.47\text{ m}$ ($34.3\text{ ft}$)**
- **Zone Breakdown:**
  - Staircase + Back Counter Area: $\sim 1.5\text{ m}$
  - Kitchen Work & Island Zone: $\sim 2.3\text{ m}$ (Counter depth: $60\text{ cm}$; Island length: $\sim 2.54\text{ m}$ seating 4 stools)
  - Dining Zone (with clearances): $\sim 3.8\text{ m}$ (10-seater dining table length: $\sim 3.01\text{ m}$)
  - Living Room Zone (sofa to TV wall): $\sim 5.7\text{ m}$
- **Front Office (Γραφείο):** $3.45\text{ m} \times 2.70\text{ m}$ ($\approx 9.3\text{ m}^2$), accommodating a $140\text{–}150\text{ cm} \times 60\text{ cm}$ desk. Overlooks the enclosed front courtyard.
- **Guest Restroom (WC Ξένων):** Powder room adjacent to the main entrance foyer (*Χώρος Εισόδου*).
- **Laundry / Storage Room (Αποθήκη):** Positioned between the public zone and private bedroom hallway to act as an acoustic buffer and potentially house the central server rack.

### 🛏️ Private Bedroom Spine
- **Master Suite (Υπνοδωμάτιο Μάστερ):** Located at the quiet terminus of the corridor; features an en-suite shower/WC and private rear deck with cascading steps down to the pool area.
- **Secondary Bedrooms (Υπνοδωμάτιο 1 & 2):** Two bedrooms ($3.50\text{ m}$ wide each) along the corridor.
- **Shared Bathrooms:** Two bathrooms (*Μπάνιο 1* and *Μπάνιο 2*) serving the secondary bedrooms.

### 🌿 Exterior & Outdoor Zones
- **Parking:** 3 designated parking bays (*ΧΣ1, ΧΣ2, ΧΣ3*) on the front-left driveway.
- **Front Courtyard & Planters (Ανθώνας):**
  - Existing **olive tree** (*Υφιστάμενο Ελαιόδεντρο - Παραμένει*) preserved outside the boundary wall within the $3\text{ m}$ setback zone.
  - Paved enclosed courtyard (*Ακάλυπτη Βεράντα*, $\sim 5.80\text{ m} \times 3.45\text{ m}$) inside the front boundary wall right outside the office—ideal for a foldable weatherproof **table tennis table**, minimalist morning coffee/decompression lounge, or potted architectural planters.
- **Covered Veranda (Καλυμμένη Βεράντα) & Backyard:** Features built-in BBQ area, outdoor dining & lounge, wall TV & ceiling projector setup, wooden decking, and surrounding lawn (*Κήπος*).
- **Swimming Pool (Πισίνα):**
  - **Overall Dimensions:** **$10.00\text{ m} \times 2.50\text{ m}$** ($1000\text{ cm} \times 250\text{ cm}$)
  - **Inner Water Basin Width:** $2.15\text{ m}$ ($215\text{ cm}$) with raised overflow channels bringing total width to $2.50\text{ m}$.
- **Auxiliary House (Βοηθητικό Σπίτι):** Fully independent secondary structure provisioned with dedicated water, drainage, electrical sub-panel, and Cat6 network link.

## 4. Smart Home, Network & Electrical Infrastructure

### 🖥️ Central Server Cabinet (Laundry Room or Office)
- **Power Circuit:** Dedicated **16A or 32A circuit** from the main distribution board feeding a rack PDU with multiple outlets.
- **Rack Equipment:**
  - Minix Mini PC (running **Home Assistant**)
  - Dedicated Firewall Appliance / Mini PC
  - PoE Network Switch
  - Reolink NVR (Camera Recorder)
  - CytaBox (ISP Modem/Router)
  - Active 1U Rack Exhaust Fans + room active exhaust ventilation ducting
- **Structured Cabling (Cat6):**
  - **Cat6 cabling** throughout (10 Gbps capable, avoiding Cat7/8 shielding/grounding pitfalls).
  - **Wi-Fi Access Points (PoE):** Ceiling drops in the central open-plan living area, bedroom corridor, and covered veranda.
  - **Zigbee / Matter Coordinator:** Cat6 run to the central entrance/living ceiling for an external PoE coordinator (e.g., SMLight) to avoid metal rack signal attenuation.
  - **Rooftop Links:** Exterior-grade FTP Cat6 to the roof for a Ubiquiti wireless link, solar PV inverter communication, and roof water tank temperature probe (ESP32/Shelly UNI).

### ⚡ Switches, Relays, Curtains & HVAC Control
- **Deep Back-Boxes:** Minimum **$6\text{ cm}$ depth** at all wall switches and roller shutter switches to house **Shelly smart relays** (Shelly 1PM / 2PM).
- **Mandatory Neutral Wires:** Neutral wire required at every switch box across the house.
- **Central Wall Touch Panel:** Eye-level Cat6 PoE drop in the main open-plan living/kitchen area for a central Smart Home Touch Panel (none required in bedrooms).
- **HVAC & Underfloor Heating:** Integration via **Dry Contacts** or **Modbus** (no KNX) so HVAC and underfloor heating can be controlled directly by Home Assistant.
- **Motorized Shutters & Curtains:** 3-core power cabling to window/door lintels wired to deep wall boxes for Shelly 2PM control, plus power provisions for motorized curtains in the open-plan living/dining room.

### 🛡️ Security, Sensors & Audio-Visual
- **Smart Sensors:**
  - **Smoke Detection:** Aqara Zigbee smoke detector mounted on the ceiling above the kitchen island (battery-powered).
  - **Temperature Sensors:** Aqara Zigbee sensors placed centrally in the hallway/living area (battery-powered).
  - **Motion/Presence:** Aqara FP300 mmWave/PIR presence sensors (battery-powered via 2× CR2450 coin cells, or high-level 220V/5V/12V corner drops if wired mmWave units are used, plus 1–2 blind high-level power boxes for future expansion).
- **Hardwired Security Provisions:**
  - Recessed wired magnetic reed contacts on all exterior sliding doors and windows (used for intrusion alerts and automatic HVAC shutoff when doors stay open).
  - Hardwired 12V ceiling/wall drops for **acoustic glass-break sensors** facing the large pool-facing sliding glass doors.
  - **PoE Cameras (Reolink):** Exterior corner eaves Cat6 drops covering parking bays, entrance gate, BBQ/pool, and rear master boundary.
  - **Dual PoE Smart Doorbells:** Cat6 runs for **two (2) PoE Smart Doorbells**—one at the outer street gate (*καγκελόπορτα*) with dry-contact remote release, and one at the main front entrance door.
  - **Sirens & Backup:** 12V indoor and outdoor sirens backed by UPS/battery so alarms remain active if the main breaker trips.
- **Room-Specific & Outdoor AV/Power:**
  - **Office (Γραφείο):** High-capacity 16A power outlets and multiple Cat6 drops installed on **both opposing walls** to allow flexible desk placement.
  - **Bedrooms:** Cat6 behind every TV position + two-way (*allé-retour*) bedside lighting switches.
  - **Covered Veranda:** Center-ceiling 2× power outlets, HDMI, and 2× Cat6 for a ceiling projector; weatherproof wall power + Cat6 for outdoor TV; ceiling provisions for **fans** and **infrared ceiling heaters**; oxygen-free speaker wire to ceilings in the living room, kitchen, and veranda (over BBQ/dining).
  - **Exterior Lighting & Switched Outlet:** Perimeter wall lighting, entrance/planter lighting, floodlights, **raised pool overflow edge lighting**, and a front exterior wall power socket controlled by an indoor switch.
  - **EV Charger (Immediate Requirement):** 3-phase power line from the main panel to the parking area for immediate electric vehicle charging (EV already owned).

## 5. Plumbing, Water Filtration & Kitchen Engineering

### 🚰 Under-Sink Kitchen System & Dispensing
- **4× Dedicated 13A Sockets Under Sink:**
  1. 6-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) filtration with **UV sterilizer lamp** (replaces standalone Midea dispenser filters)
  2. Under-sink compressor chiller & carbonator (e.g., **Quooker CUBE** or **Grohe Blue**) cooling water and injecting $\text{CO}_2$ for sparkling water on tap
  3. Boiling water tap boiler (e.g., **Quooker** or **Grohe Red**) for $100^\circ\text{C}$ instant boiling water
  4. Garbage disposal (*Σκουπιδοφάγος*) controlled via countertop air-switch or wall switch
- **Dual-Line RO T-Splitter:**
  - **Line 1:** Feeds the multi-way smart kitchen mixer tap for boiling, chilled, and sparkling water.
  - **Line 2:** Runs via a $1/4\text{"}$ tube behind the cabinetry directly to the refrigerator for pure crystal-clear ice cubes and chilled water (bypassing proprietary internal fridge filters).
- **Cabinet Allocation:** Dedicated $60\text{ cm}$ cabinet space adjacent to waste bins to house the RO tank, chiller/carbonator, boiler, and $\text{CO}_2$ cylinder.

### 🚿 Whole-House Water & Exterior Plumbing
- **Mains Entry Filtration & Softening:** Exterior mechanical room space with a waterproof 13A socket and floor drain for a high-flow **Sediment Filter** (*Φίλτρο στερεών σωματιδίων*) and central **Water Softener** (*Αποσκληρυντής*) to protect plumbing, showers, and solar panels from limescale (*άλατα*).
- **Hot Water Recirculation:** Dedicated hot water return/recirculation loop (*Ανακυκλοφορία Ζεστού Νερού*) for instant hot water at all taps.
- **Outdoor Poolside Facilities:** Full plumbing (hot/cold water, drainage) and electrical provisions for an **exterior toilet with sink** and an **outdoor shower** near the pool, plus water/drainage/13A power at the BBQ counter, perimeter planter irrigation taps (*πότισμα ανθώνα*), and a car-wash tap at the parking area.
- **Pool Plant Room:** Power supply + heavy-gauge cabling provision for a future pool **Heat Pump**.

## 6. Town Planning Permit Conditions (Πολεοδομική Άδεια - Λατσιά)

> [!warning] Regulatory Compliance (File No. 01.100.7502 / Εντολή 2/2020)
> - **Condition 500 (Geotechnical Study):** Prior to submitting the Building Permit (*Άδεια Οικοδομής*) application, a formal geological/geotechnical investigation must be conducted by an **ETEK-certified Geologist** (Seismic Zone 2) to assess geo-hazards, excavation, and foundation requirements. Any required foundation changes will require an amended planning application.
> - **Setbacks:**
>   - Main building, uncovered verandas, and earth fills $>1.20\text{ m}$ must respect the **$3.00\text{ m}$** setback.
>   - **Swimming Pool:** Minimum **$1.50\text{ m}$** from neighboring boundaries and **$3.00\text{ m}$** from the road boundary, with perimeter tree planting. Pool must **not** be filled directly from the public water supply network.
>   - **BBQ / Oven:** Minimum **$1.80\text{ m}$** from property boundaries and **$3.00\text{ m}$** from the road boundary.
> - **Boundary Walls & Parapets:** Street-side boundary walls max **$1.20\text{ m}$** height within the first $3.00\text{ m}$ from the road, and up to **$2.10\text{ m}$** elsewhere. Balcony/roof parapets min **$1.10\text{ m}$** clear height.
> - **Roof Equipment:** Solar water heater tanks max **$1.60\text{ m}$** above roof level. Flat-roof PV panels max **$1.20\text{ m}$** height and set back from the roof edge by at least their height.
> - **Public Infrastructure:** Existing pavements/roads must be restored post-construction; underground utility sleeves (EAC, Water Board, CYTA) must be installed prior to major works.
